## Notes

**Why January.** Because January is winter, and winter is the most beautiful thing there is. My hometown is Varanasi, in the north, and a winter morning there, fog on the river, the cold, the first tea, is where this film begins and where it comes back to. The circle of the year is a circle of winters.

**The shape.** The film runs one year, January to January. Each month is set in a different state of India, chosen because that is where the season looks most like itself. Winter is a snowy hill station. The monsoon is the rainforests of the south. India, in all its weathers, is the backdrop for the romance.

**The heart.** The dreams two people see together, and the small ones most of all: the ones both knew were achievable, that do get achieved, after the two of them are no longer together.
