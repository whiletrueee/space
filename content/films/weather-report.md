## Notes

**The shape.** The film runs one year, starting in January and ending in January. Each month is set in a different state of India, chosen because that is where the season looks most like itself. Winter is a snowy hill station. The monsoon is the rainforests of the south. India, in all its weathers, is the backdrop for the romance.

**The heart.** The dreams two people see together, and the small ones most of all: the ones both knew were achievable, that do get achieved, after the two of them are no longer together.
